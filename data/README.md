# Region pack

`region.pmtiles` holds building outlines and house numbers from [Overture Maps](https://overturemaps.org)
(release 2026-10-21.0) for Chino, Chino Hills, Ontario, Eastvale, Norco and all of Corona
(lon -117.78 to -117.43, lat 33.76 to 34.10). It fills in neighborhoods OpenStreetMap hasn't mapped yet.

Rebuild or move it to another area with `tools/build_region.py` in the Knock project.
